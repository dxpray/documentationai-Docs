---
title: How Rose Handles Replies
description: Before a conversation reaches your Inbox, Rose has already read it, understood it, and decided what to do.
---

For every candidate reply, Rose reads the message and reasons about it before anything reaches you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Checks it's genuine" icon="shield-check">
    Rose filters out auto-responders, spam, and attempts to manipulate her through email content — so noise never reaches you and never derails a campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reads the intent" icon="message-circle">
    Rose works out what the candidate means — interested, asking questions, not interested, or asking to opt out — and moves them to the right pipeline stage automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pulls out availability" icon="calendar-clock">
    If the candidate suggests times, Rose extracts the dates, times, and time zone (inferring it from their location if needed) and kicks off booking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Responds in your voice" icon="reply">
    Rose drafts a reply — answering questions and, when appropriate, inviting the candidate to book — then sends it, queues it for your approval, or steps aside, depending on your settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Opt-outs are honoured automatically. If a candidate asks to be removed, Rose stops contacting them and suppresses future outreach — you don't need to do a thing.
</Callout>

How much Rose sends on her own depends on the project's reply mode — which you choose at launch and can change any time with **Reply automation** on the project's **Job Details** tab:

- **Rose replies automatically** — Rose handles the conversation end to end and escalates only the tricky ones.
- **Rose drafts, you approve** — Rose's drafts wait in your Inbox for you to review before they go out.
- **You reply yourself** — Rose stays out of the thread; every reply comes from you.

See [Levels of Control](/how-rose-works/levels-of-control) for more. This is why the Inbox stays quiet: anything Rose can handle, she handles, and only the conversations that genuinely need a human land in front of you.
