This page is the full tour of what Rose can do, in the order she uses it across a hire. For the short version, see [How Rose Works](/how-rose-works); for her skills with example prompts, see [What Rose Can Do](/how-rose-works/skills).

Rose is an **agent**, not a search box. She works a brief across many steps, the same way a recruiter moves between researching companies, shortlisting people, writing outreach, and chasing replies — and she checks in with you at every decision that matters.

## Planning the hire

New chats start in **Planning** mode. Describe the role and Rose proposes a step-by-step plan — understand the role, explore the market, find candidates, build the shortlist, reach out. Nothing happens until you click **Run with me**, and she still checks in at each step. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).

## Understanding your brief

- **Natural-language intake.** Describe the role however you'd brief a colleague, or upload a job description (PDF, DOCX or TXT) and Rose fills in the details from it.
- **Questions on cards.** When something's missing — location, compensation, must-haves — Rose asks, usually several questions on one card, with suggested answers you can tap. Answer in any order; she keeps the details on the project as you go.
- **Drafting the job description.** No JD yet? Choose **Draft with me** and Rose writes it with you.
- **Your company, built in.** If you've filled out your [Knowledge Base](/getting-started/knowledge-base), Rose carries your company's industry, stage, size, and story into every chat — so you never have to repeat yourself.

See [Creating a Project](/projects/creating-a-project).

## Finding candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sizes up the market" icon="building-2">
    Rose can size a talent pool, map the companies your ideal candidate works at today, and find companies like the ones you name — competitors and adjacent players — before you commit to a search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calibrates with you" icon="user-check">
    Rose shows you three candidates at a time. Mark each **Good match**, **Not a match**, or **Unsure** — and say why — and the next three come back tuned to your verdicts. Once a round lands, you accept the search and can pull more candidates from it whenever the project needs people. See [Calibrating a Search](/finding-candidates/calibration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or searches in real time" icon="search">
    When you already know exactly who you want — named companies, a specific title — Rose searches live and groups the results into named clusters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Rose can also **search the web** for fresh context, such as a company's recent news or funding, and shows you the sources she used.

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Relevant experience, not raw totals.** Rose shows each candidate's relevant years of experience — excluding internships and time in unrelated roles — so the number reflects experience that actually counts.
</Callout>

## Shaping the outreach

Rose asks **How should the outreach run?** and gives you a builder to shape the sequence from steps — **Email**, **LinkedIn invite**, **LinkedIn message**, and **InMail**. Start from a template (**Email only**, **LinkedIn only**, **Email + LinkedIn**, or **From a project**), adjust the steps and waits, then review every message she writes.

- **Personalised for every candidate.** Rose writes each step for the person and the role, including a personal intro you can edit.
- **Deliverability by design.** Phrases that tend to land in spam are highlighted; remove them yourself or use **Fix all with Rose**.

See [The Outreach Sequence](/projects/outreach-sequence).

## Sending from your own accounts

Outreach goes out from **you**, so replies come back to you:

- **Email** sends from your own connected mailbox — Gmail, Outlook, or another inbox (connecting a mailbox is rolling out to workspaces gradually; until then Rose sends for you). For an email-only sequence you can choose **Let Rose send it** instead. See [Mailboxes](/integrations/mailboxes).
- **LinkedIn** steps send from your own LinkedIn account. See [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin).
- **Paced sending.** Messages go out gradually inside your sending schedule, and Rose pauses automatically if emails start bouncing.

## Launching

Rose checks your connections, asks how candidate replies should be handled, shows you a final review, and launches when you click **Launch project** — enriching contact details on the way out. You can keep adding candidates to a running project. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching).

## Holding the conversation

When a candidate replies, Rose reads and reasons about the message:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Is this a real reply?" icon="shield-check">
    Rose filters out auto-responders, spam, and manipulation attempts before anything else happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What does the candidate mean?" icon="message-circle">
    Rose works out the intent — interested, asking questions, not interested, or asking to opt out — and updates the candidate's pipeline stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Did they share availability?" icon="calendar-clock">
    Rose picks out any dates, times, and time zone from the reply. If a time zone isn't stated, she infers it from the candidate's location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="She responds" icon="reply">
    Rose drafts a reply in your voice — answering questions and, when appropriate, inviting the candidate to book — then sends it, waits for your approval, or steps aside, depending on the reply mode you've chosen. See [Your Level of Control](/how-rose-works/levels-of-control).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  Opt-outs are always honoured automatically. If a candidate asks to be removed, Rose stops contacting them — no action needed from you.
</Callout>

## Booking the interview

When a candidate is ready, Rose closes the loop:

- **Finds mutual availability** by matching the candidate's stated times against your connected calendar.
- **Respects your rules** — buffers between meetings, minimum notice, and a daily cap on bookings.
- **Books the call** — creating the calendar event, attaching a video link, and confirming with both you and the candidate.

The **Scheduling Mode** in your [Interview Preferences](/integrations/interview-preferences) decides how much she does:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Automatic" icon="zap">
    Rose books interviews automatically on the first available slot.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Semi-automatic" icon="list-checks">
    Rose suggests interview times. You confirm or pick another.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual" icon="hand">
    Rose notifies you when a candidate is ready. You book the interview yourself.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Keeping you informed

- **The Inbox** shows your candidate conversations, with **Needs you** for the ones waiting on you. See [Inbox](/inbox).
- **Notifications** reach you by email and, if connected, in **Slack** when a candidate is ready.
- **Project analytics** show the funnel for every project. See [Project Analytics](/project-analytics).

## Working safely

- **Rose never adds candidates without your say-so.** She shows you profiles first and only adds people when you ask — approving a plan never covers it.
- **Nothing sends before launch.** You approve the outreach sequence and click **Launch project** yourself.
- **Hardened against manipulation.** Rose treats candidate messages as untrusted and is protected against attempts to hijack a project through email content.
- **Honest about limits.** Rose is AI and can make mistakes — give profiles and important replies a glance before they go out.
