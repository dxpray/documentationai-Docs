Hiros gives you three ways to find candidates:

- **[Rose AI Search](/finding-candidates/rose-ai-search)** — describe who you want in plain language and let Rose do the research. When you already know exactly who you want, such as named companies or a specific title, she searches in real time and groups the results into named clusters.
- **[Calibration](/finding-candidates/calibration)** — Rose shows you three candidates at a time and tunes the next three to your verdicts, until the search is right. The best route when "a good fit" is hard to put into filters.
- **[Manual Filters](/finding-candidates/manual-filters)** — build a precise search yourself across role, skills, location, company, experience, and education.

Whichever you use, the candidates you choose are added to a [project](/projects), where Rose takes over outreach. Rose only adds people when you ask her to.

## Which should I use?

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with Rose. Calibration reasons about fit rather than just matching keywords, and you can steer her in conversation. Reach for Manual Filters when you want exact, hands-on control.
</Callout>

You'll find Rose on the home screen (**Rose** in the left sidebar). To open Manual Filters, click **Manual Search** under the message box.
