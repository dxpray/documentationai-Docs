Your plan is shown at the top of **Plans & credits** (Settings → Billing), with its price, your remaining credits and the renewal date.

## Comparing plans

Click **Compare plans** (or **Upgrade** if you don't have a plan yet) to see your options side by side. Each plan shows its price per seat, its monthly credit allowance and what's included.

- **Monthly or yearly** — the comparison opens on monthly billing. Switch to **Yearly** to see the yearly prices, which work out cheaper.
- **Seats** — paid plans are priced per seat. Choose how many seats you need; it starts at the number of people already in your organisation.
- **Bigger teams** — need something bespoke? Use **Talk to sales**, or **Contact sales** on the Enterprise option.

You can change or cancel your plan at any time. **Manage billing** opens your invoices, payment method and billing details.

## What your plan determines

- **Your monthly credit allowance** — shared between your seats. See [Credits](/billing-and-subscription/credits).
- **Mailboxes per team member** — how many mailboxes each person can connect for outreach. The Integrations page shows how many you've used.
- **ATS integrations** — included on some plans.

LinkedIn outreach is available on all plans.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Reached a limit? Anything your plan doesn't include shows **Upgrade to unlock** on the Integrations page. Upgrade and it unlocks straight away — if you're not an admin, ask an organisation admin to upgrade.
</Callout>
