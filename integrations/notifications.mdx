Connect **Slack** so Rose can keep you posted where your team already works.

| Integration | What it does |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Slack** | Rose notifies you in Slack when new candidates are ready for review, and when a candidate meets a project's goal if you turn that on. |

## Setting it up

Click **Connect** on the Slack card and authorise the connection. Once linked, choose the **Slack Channel** where Rose should post. Rose joins the channel so she can send messages — you'll see *“Rose has joined the channel.”* when it's set.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Don't see the channel you want? Create it in Slack first, then come back and pick it from the list.
</Callout>

## When a goal is met

Choose **Email me**, **Post in Slack**, or both under **When a goal is met** in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings). You hear about each candidate once, with what they shared. See [Reply Objectives](/how-rose-works/reply-objectives).
