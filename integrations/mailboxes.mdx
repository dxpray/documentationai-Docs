Outreach lands best when it comes from a real person's inbox. Connect your mailbox and Rose sends your projects' emails from it, tracks replies, and keeps every thread in your [Inbox](/inbox).

<Callout kind="info">
  Connecting your own mailbox is rolling out to workspaces gradually. If you don't see a **Mailboxes** section on your Integrations page yet, nothing is missing — Rose sends your projects' emails for you.
</Callout>

## Connecting a mailbox

Go to **Integrations** (under **More** in the sidebar) and find **Mailboxes**. Choose your provider:

| Provider | Use it for |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Gmail** | Google Workspace or Gmail accounts |
| **Outlook** | Microsoft 365 or Outlook accounts |
| **IMAP** | Any other mailbox, using your IMAP/SMTP details |

Sign in with the provider, approve access, and you're returned to Hiros. Use **Add another mailbox** to connect more, up to what your plan includes.

<Callout kind="tip">
  You don't have to leave the chat. When Rose is setting up an email sequence and you haven't connected an inbox, she shows **Connect your inbox to send these emails** with the same three options — and you come straight back to the conversation afterwards.
</Callout>

### Not ready to connect one?

For an **email-only** sequence you can choose **Let Rose send it**, and Rose sends the emails for you. Sequences that mix email and LinkedIn always send email from your own inbox.

## Mailboxes are personal

Each mailbox belongs to the person who connected it, and a project sends from **its owner's** mailbox. Teammates can't see or use your connected address, and an inbox that's already connected by someone else on Hiros can't be connected again.

If you're working on a teammate's project, Rose will tell you when the owner needs to connect their inbox — connecting your own wouldn't change it.

## How much you can connect

The Mailboxes section shows your plan's allowance — for example *1 of 2 on your plan*, or *Unlimited on your plan*. Allowances are per team member. If you've reached it, you'll be asked to upgrade before adding another. See [Subscription Plans](/billing-and-subscription/plans).

Each mailbox shows its daily sending limit. Rose paces sends gradually within it, and inside each project's sending schedule.

## If a mailbox disconnects

If a mailbox is disconnected — a password change, revoked access — Rose **pauses** every project sending from it so no candidate misses a step. You'll see *Sending is paused* on the project, and the owner gets an email. Click **Reconnect mailbox**, then **Resume** the project.
