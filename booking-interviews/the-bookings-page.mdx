Click **Bookings** in the left sidebar to open **Your Interviews** — every scheduled interview in one place.

## Summary and filters

The top of the page shows quick counts: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This week**, **Confirmed** (with your confirmation rate), and **Past**. Use the **Upcoming / Past / All** tabs to switch views; upcoming interviews are grouped into Today, Tomorrow, This week, Next week, and Later.

## Each interview

Every row shows the time, the candidate's name and headline, the duration, and the meeting provider, along with a status. Quick buttons let you open the calendar event and join the call; once a call is completed, a transcript link appears if one is available. Click a row to open the candidate's profile.

For upcoming interviews, the row's menu offers **Reschedule**, **Change duration**, and **Cancel Interview**.

## Up next and Today

A side panel highlights what's **Up next** — your closest upcoming call, with a countdown, **Join call**, and **Copy link** — and lists everything on for **Today**.

## Calendar health

The **Calendar health** panel is a live status check of your connected tools — Google Calendar, Zoom, and Outlook — showing which are connected and which aren't.

<Callout kind="warning">
  If Calendar health shows a calendar isn't connected, connect it on the [Integrations](/integrations/scheduling) page. Rose can't book interviews without an active calendar connection.
</Callout>
