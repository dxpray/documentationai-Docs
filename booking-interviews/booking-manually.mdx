---
title: Booking Manually
description: Pick a time yourself, from an Inbox conversation or a candidate's profile in your project.
---

You can book a call yourself from two places:

- **The Inbox** — when a candidate has shared their availability, click **Book interview** in the thread header. See [Managing Conversations](/inbox/managing-conversations).
- **Your project** — open the candidate's profile from the project's **Leads (CRM)** tab and click **Book Intro Call**.

## The booking flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time" icon="calendar">
    Choose a date, then a time slot. Available times are pulled from your connected calendar, and you can set the meeting duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the details" icon="check-circle">
    Review the candidate, date, time, and timezone. Optionally add notes for the candidate — prep tips or topics for the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done" icon="party-popper">
    Click **Confirm Booking**. The event is added to your calendar (with a Zoom link if you've connected Zoom), and the candidate receives a confirmation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After confirming, you'll see **Booking Confirmed!** along with a link to open the calendar event.

<Callout kind="info">
  Need to change a booking? You can **Reschedule** or cancel an interview from the [Bookings page](/booking-interviews/the-bookings-page).
</Callout>
