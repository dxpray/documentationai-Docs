---
title: How Rose Books Interviews
description: Rose matches candidate availability against your calendar and books the call — automatically, semi-automatically, or by handing it to you.
---

When a candidate shares availability, Rose works out exactly when the call should happen. She compares the candidate's stated times against your connected calendar, finds the windows where you're both free, and applies your scheduling rules before proposing or confirming anything.

## The rules Rose follows

Rose respects the guardrails you set in your [Interview Preferences](/integrations/interview-preferences):

- **Availability** — the days and hours you're open to interview.
- **Buffers** between back-to-back calls.
- **Minimum notice** before a call can be booked.
- **A daily cap** on how many interviews can be scheduled.
- **How far ahead** Rose can schedule.
- **Time zones** — Rose works out the candidate's time zone from what they wrote (or their location) and matches it against your availability.

## The three scheduling modes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Automatic" icon="zap">
    Rose books the first slot where you're both free and confirms it immediately — calendar invite, video link, and confirmations to both sides, with no action needed from you.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Semi-automatic" icon="list-checks">
    Rose suggests interview times and waits for you to confirm or pick another before booking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual" icon="hand">
    Rose notifies you when a candidate is ready and hands off, so you book the time yourself.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When Rose books

Rose books only on projects where she replies automatically and the objective is **Ask for availability**. Whatever the settings, the times a candidate gives are always saved.

| Project setup | What happens when a candidate shares times |
| :---- | :---- |
| Rose replies automatically, scheduling mode **Automatic** | Rose books the call and you get a *Call scheduled* email. |
| Rose replies automatically, scheduling mode **Semi-automatic** | You get an email to pick a slot. |
| Rose replies automatically, scheduling mode **Manual** or no preferences set | You get an email with the times, and you book the call. |
| Rose drafts, you approve, or you've taken over the thread | You get an email with the times, and you book the call. |
| You reply yourself | Nothing is sent. The conversation stays with you in the [Inbox](/inbox). |

Emails follow the project owner's [notification settings](/how-rose-works/rose-settings) and go out once per candidate.

<Callout kind="info">
  If a candidate shares their availability on LinkedIn but Rose doesn't have their email address, she can't send them a calendar invite. Your email says so, and you can book them over LinkedIn instead.
</Callout>
