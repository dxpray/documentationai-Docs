---
title: Billing & Subscription
description: Manage your plan, credits, seats and billing details from Settings.
---

Manage everything money-related from **Settings → Billing**, titled **Plans & credits**. You'll reach it from **More → Settings**, or by clicking the credits ring at the bottom of the left sidebar.

On this page you can:

- See your current plan and how many credits are left this period.
- Add **Team seats** and invite teammates (admins).
- Decide **how credits are shared** between seats (admins).
- **Compare plans** to upgrade or change plan, and open **Manage billing** for invoices, payment method and billing details.

Learn more:

- **[Credits](/billing-and-subscription/credits)** — what uses credits, how they're shared, and what to do when you run low.
- **[Subscription Plans](/billing-and-subscription/plans)** — comparing plans, seats and plan limits.
- **[Inviting Your Team](/multi-user-management/inviting-your-team)** — adding seats and teammates.
