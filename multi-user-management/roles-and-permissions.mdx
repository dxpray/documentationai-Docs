Every member of your organisation is either an **admin** or a **member**. Roles are shown in your organisation's member list — open the **organisation switcher** at the bottom of the left sidebar to see them, change a role, or remove someone.

## Admins and members

**Members** run their own hiring: they chat with Rose, create and launch projects, connect their own mailbox and LinkedIn account, and work their Inbox and Bookings.

**Admins** can do everything a member can, plus:

- Add or reduce **Team seats** and invite teammates.
- Decide **how credits are shared** between seats.
- Choose their own **Project visibility** (see below).

## Projects are private by default

A project belongs to the person who created it. Nobody else sees it — or its candidates, conversations and bookings — until it's shared.

To share a project, open it and click **Share**. In **Share this project**, add a teammate by name. The project then shows as **Shared** instead of **Only you**, and you can remove people the same way.

<Callout kind="info">
  Org admins can also open a project when needed. It shows as admin access, never silently, and that access is logged.
</Callout>

## Project visibility (admins)

Admins choose how much of the workspace they see from **Settings → Project visibility**. Turn on **Show all projects in the workspace** to see every project, chat and inbox thread in the organisation — useful for oversight and for helping teammates. Leave it off for a focused view: your own projects plus any a teammate has shared with you.

## Personal connections

Mailboxes and LinkedIn accounts belong to the person who connected them. A project sends from its owner's accounts, and only the owner of a LinkedIn account can change its **Sending limits**.
