## Settings

Open **Settings** from **More** in the left sidebar. It has these pages:

- **Billing** — your plan, credits, Team seats and how credits are shared. See [Billing & Subscription](/billing-and-subscription).
- **Integrations** — your [interview preferences](/integrations/interview-preferences) for each connected calendar. To connect mailboxes, LinkedIn, calendars, Slack or an ATS, use **More → Integrations** (see [Integrations](/integrations)).
- **Knowledge base** — what Rose knows about your company. She uses it to seed every chat.
- **Project visibility** — admins only. Choose whether you see every project in the workspace or just your own and those shared with you. See [Roles & Permissions](/multi-user-management/roles-and-permissions).

## The organisation switcher

The **organisation switcher** sits at the bottom of the left sidebar. Click it to switch between organisations you belong to, or to manage the current one:

- **Members** — see who's in the organisation, change roles, and remove people. To add people, use **Invite teammates** on Settings → Billing (see [Inviting Your Team](/multi-user-management/inviting-your-team)).
- **General** — update the organisation's name and logo, leave the organisation, or delete it.
- **About** — your company details: **Company Name**, a short **Description**, and **Recruiter Account**, which you turn on if you recruit on behalf of other businesses.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Deleting an organisation is irreversible and removes all associated data. Make sure this is what you intend before confirming.
</Callout>
