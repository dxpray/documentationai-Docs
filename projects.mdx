A **Project** is one role you're hiring for. It holds the job details, the pipeline of candidates, the outreach sequence Rose sends, and every conversation and booking that comes out of it. Click **Projects** in the left sidebar to see yours.

<Callout kind="info">
  Projects used to be called **Sequences**. Nothing about your existing work has changed — only the name.
</Callout>

## The Projects page

The page opens with **Your Projects** and four headline stats:

| Stat | What it shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Active** | How many projects are currently running |
| **Candidates** | How many candidates have been contacted so far |
| **Reply rate** | The share of contacted candidates who replied |
| **Interviews booked** | Interviews booked across non-cancelled projects |

Below that, each project appears as a row with its creator, location, replies, reply rate, who it's shared with, and its status. Use **Filter** to view by status, and **New Project** to start a new one.

## Who can see a project

Projects are **private to the person who created them** unless they're shared. Share a project with teammates to work on it together. Organisation admins can choose whether they see every project in the workspace — see [Roles & Permissions](/multi-user-management/roles-and-permissions).
