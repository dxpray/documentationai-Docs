Hiros helps you reach top candidates for your open roles. At the centre of it is **Rose** — an AI recruiter who finds real candidates, reaches out from your own accounts, and handles the conversation until someone is ready to speak to your team.

## Before you start

Setup takes a few minutes:

- [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account) and your organisation.
- [Connect your calendar](/getting-started/connect-calendar) so Rose can book intro calls.
- [Set up your Knowledge Base](/getting-started/knowledge-base) so Rose knows your company.

## Your first project in five steps

Everything starts on the Rose home screen. Click **Rose** in the left sidebar and type in the message box.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-6838a7a8-9bb8-4a3f-bda5-450ea34dc262/doc-c7e6ca58-4630-4814-b064-42b77b2b78ad/1791067537534-vg4736fhe1-rose-home.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="2880" height="1520" alt="The Rose home screen with the message box, the project picker and the Browse skills link" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Brief Rose" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Describe the role in a sentence, or upload a job description. New chats start in **Planning** mode, so Rose proposes a step-by-step plan before she does anything. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Answer her questions" icon="list-checks" title-type="p">
    Rose asks for what she still needs — location, compensation, must-haves, the job description — and creates the project as you go. See [Creating a Project](/projects/creating-a-project).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Calibrate the search" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Rose shows you three candidates at a time. Mark each a good match or not, and she tunes the next three until the search is right. See [Calibrating a Search](/finding-candidates/calibration).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shape the outreach sequence" icon="mail" title-type="p">
    Choose email, LinkedIn, or both, then review every message Rose writes. See [The Outreach Sequence](/projects/outreach-sequence).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch" icon="rocket" title-type="p">
    Connect your mailbox or LinkedIn, choose how replies are handled, and click **Launch project**. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching).
  </Step>
</Steps>

From there, replies arrive in your [Inbox](/inbox) and interested candidates move towards a [booked interview](/booking-interviews).

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  Not sure where to start? Click **Browse skills** under the message box to see everything Rose can do, with example prompts. See [What Rose Can Do](/how-rose-works/skills).
</Callout>

## Finding your way around

| Item         | What it's for                                                  |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rose**     | The home screen — chat with Rose and start new work            |
| **Projects** | One project per role: candidates, outreach, and job details    |
| **Inbox**    | Candidate conversations, with drafts waiting for your approval |
| **Bookings** | Your scheduled interviews                                      |
| **More**     | Integrations, Settings, and this documentation                 |

Your recent chats are listed under the main items. Your credit balance sits at the bottom of the sidebar — click it to open **Plans & credits**. See [Credits](/billing-and-subscription/credits).
