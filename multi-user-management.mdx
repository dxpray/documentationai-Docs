---
title: Multi-User Management
description: Add your colleagues to Hiros so your whole team can collaborate on hiring.
---

Hiros is built for teams. Everyone in your organisation shares the same plan, credits and Knowledge base, while each person keeps their own projects, Rose chats, mailbox and LinkedIn account. Projects are private by default — you choose who to share them with.

- **[Inviting Your Team](/multi-user-management/inviting-your-team)** — add seats and invite colleagues from Settings → Billing.
- **[Roles & Permissions](/multi-user-management/roles-and-permissions)** — what admins and members can do, and how project sharing works.
- **[Organisation Settings](/multi-user-management/organisation-settings)** — your Settings pages, your organisation profile and the organisation switcher.

<Callout kind="info">
  The **organisation switcher** sits at the bottom of the left sidebar. Click it to manage your organisation and its members, or to switch between organisations you belong to.
</Callout>
