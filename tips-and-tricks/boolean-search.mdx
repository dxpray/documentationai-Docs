---
title: Boolean Search
description: Combine terms with AND and OR in Manual Search to build precise, flexible queries.
---

In [Manual Search](/finding-candidates/manual-filters), these fields can combine their values with Boolean operators:

- **Current title** and **Past title**
- **Current company** and **Past company**
- **Keywords**

## How it works

Add each term as its own chip — type it and press **Enter**. Between every two chips sits a small **OR** pill. Click it to switch it to **AND**, and click again to switch back.

- **OR** widens the search: a candidate needs to match *any* of the terms.
- **AND** narrows it: a candidate needs to match *all* of the terms joined by it.

## Simple searches — just use OR

For alternatives within one idea, leave every pill on OR:

```
VP  OR  Vice President  OR  EVP  OR  SVP
```

```
Account Executive  OR  AE  OR  Business Development
```

## Combining ideas — mix AND and OR

AND binds tighter than OR, so terms joined by AND are grouped together first. These chips:

```
Senior  AND  Sales  OR  Lead  AND  Business Development
```

search for *(Senior AND Sales)* **or** *(Lead AND Business Development)*.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep each chip to one term or phrase — **Account Executive** as a single chip, not two. If a combination gets complicated, it's often easier to describe it to Rose in plain language instead.
</Callout>
