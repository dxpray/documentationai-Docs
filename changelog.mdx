## Overview

This is the highlight reel of big changes to Hiros — the features that changed how you source, engage, and book candidates. We ship smaller improvements and fixes continuously between these milestones.

<Update label="September 2026" description="Planning, calibration, LinkedIn & Projects" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Sequences are now Projects

- **Projects.** What used to be called a sequence is now a **project**. The outreach sequence is the set of steps candidates receive.
- **Planning mode.** Hand Rose the whole hire: she proposes a step-by-step plan, you hit **Run with me**, and she works through it with you. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).
- **Calibration.** Rose shows you three candidates at a time — mark each **Good match**, **Not a match** or **Unsure** and she sharpens the search until it's right. See [Calibrating a Search](/finding-candidates/calibration).
- **Exclude previously sourced** candidates from a new search.
- **Personalised intros** for every candidate, which you can edit before launch.

## Reach candidates on LinkedIn

- **LinkedIn outreach.** Connect your own LinkedIn account and add LinkedIn steps to a project. See [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin).
- **Your accounts, your voice.** Mailboxes and LinkedIn accounts are per person, and each project sends from its owner's accounts.
- **Project visibility for admins.** Choose whether to see every project in the workspace or just your own and those shared with you.

## Build outreach your way

- **Sequence builder.** Pick the steps candidates receive — Email, LinkedIn invite, LinkedIn message and InMail — or start from a template: Email only, LinkedIn only, or Email + LinkedIn.
- **Browse skills.** See everything Rose can do in one place. See [What Rose Can Do](/how-rose-works/skills).
- **New projects start with Rose.** **New Project** and uploading a job description open Rose with a draft prompt, and she asks the intake questions she needs, right in the chat.
- **Recent chats** in the sidebar, with titles written for you.
- **A focused Inbox.** Filter by **Needs you**, **All** or **Archived**, filter by project, search, and archive any conversation. Rose's drafts show as **Draft ready** for you to approve, edit or reject.
- **Mailbox safety.** If a mailbox disconnects, its projects pause automatically until you **Reconnect mailbox**. LinkedIn account owners can set their own **Sending limits**.
- **Invite teammates from Billing.** Add a seat and send the invitation in one flow — their seat and credits are ready when they join. Plan limits now include mailboxes per member, and LinkedIn is available on every plan.
- **“Sourced” is now “Shortlisted”** in your pipeline.

</Update>

<Update label="July 2026" description="Team sharing & seats" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Built for teams

- **Share projects with teammates.** Projects are private to their creator — click **Share** to bring colleagues in. They get an email when you do.
- **Team seats.** Manage seats on Settings → Billing, and decide how credits are shared — an equal split by default, or a set amount per person.
- **Smarter reply handling.** Rose's reply engine was rebuilt to read candidate intent more reliably.
- **Search two locations at once.**
- **Timezone and schedule in chat.** Rose asks when and where outreach should go out as part of setting up a project.
- **Phone numbers in exports.**
- **A tidier pipeline.** The list view leads with the candidates who need action, and Hiros remembers which view you last used, across devices.

</Update>

<Update label="June 2026" description="Sequence workflow & billing" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Sequences, reimagined around Rose

- **Start a sequence straight from Rose.** The home screen now lets you pick a new or existing sequence before you start a search, so candidates flow into the right place from the first message.
- **Add candidates to ongoing sequences.** Top up a live sequence with new candidates at any time — no need to start over.
- **Editable company details** on a sequence's job details.

## Billing

- **Seat-based billing and local currency** — plans now reflect your team size and show pricing in your currency.

</Update>

<Update label="May 2026" description="Rose AI Search" tags={["feature"]}>

## Meet Rose, your conversational recruiter

- **Rose AI Search.** Describe who you're hiring in plain language and Rose researches, sources, and returns curated **Candidate Packages** — a fundamentally faster way to find people than manual filters.
- **New credit system.** A clearer, usage-based model so you can see exactly what each action costs.
- **French-language outreach.** Rose can now run outreach in French as well as English.
- **CSV contact export.** Export candidate contacts directly for use elsewhere.

</Update>

<Update label="April 2026" description="Control & setup" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## More control over how Rose works

- **Reply automation modes.** Choose how much Rose runs on her own — reply automatically, draft for your approval, or stay hands-off while you reply yourself.
- **Import a job description.** Upload a JD to auto-fill a new sequence in seconds.
- **Create and manage sequences from search.** Spin up, edit, and delete sequences as a natural part of sourcing.

</Update>

<Update label="March 2026" description="Notifications & sourcing" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>

## Stay in the loop, source more precisely

- **Slack notifications.** Rose pings you in Slack when candidates are ready for review.
- **Cancel sequences** whenever you need to stop outreach.
- **Automatic decline emails.** Candidates you pass on receive a courteous note, automatically.
- **Richer natural-language search** — target multiple companies and titles in a single brief.

</Update>

<Update label="October 2025" description="Booking & conversations" tags={["feature"]}>

## From outreach to booked calls

- **Interview booking system.** Rose turns interested candidates into scheduled intro calls on your calendar.
- **Rose handles replies.** Rose reads candidate responses, understands intent, and replies on your behalf.
- **AI-personalised outreach emails.** Every message is tailored to the candidate and the role.

</Update>
