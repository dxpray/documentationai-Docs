Open any project from the **Projects** page. The header shows its title, status and how many candidates it holds, with **Share** to bring teammates in. Underneath is a row of tabs.

## The tabs

| Tab | What's there |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Leads (CRM)** | The candidate pipeline, as a board or a list |
| **Outreach** | The sequence Rose sends and its sending schedule — see [The Outreach Sequence](/projects/outreach-sequence) |
| **Job Details** | The role details, plus the **Reply automation** setting |
| **Interviews booked** | Interviews already scheduled from this project |
| **ATS Integration** | Push candidates to your connected ATS (if your plan includes it) |

When Rose has reply drafts waiting for you, the tab bar shows how many — for example *1 draft awaiting approval*. Candidates who are ready to book sit in the **Ready to book** stage of the pipeline.

## The pipeline

On **Leads (CRM)**, candidates are grouped by where they are in the journey:

| Stage | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Shortlisted** | Added to this project — not contacted yet |
| **In Outreach** | In the outreach sequence — no reply yet |
| **Replied** | In conversation |
| **Interested** | Showed interest — Rose is moving them forward |
| **Ready to book** | Shared availability — pick a slot |
| **Interviews booked** | Interview confirmed on your calendar |
| **Email not found** | No verified address — try LinkedIn instead |
| **Not interested** | Passed on the role |
| **Rejected** | Removed from this project |

A coloured bar across the top shows how the whole project splits across stages. Switch between **Comfortable**, **Compact** and **List** views — your choice is remembered. Use **Show stages** to hide stages you don't need.

### Moving candidates

Drag a card to another stage to update it. Moving someone out of outreach stops Rose's follow-ups to them; moving someone who has already engaged back into an outreach stage lets her resume. Rose asks you to confirm either way.

To reject a candidate, choose **Reject & notify**. They move to **Rejected** and receive a polite decline email — you can add an optional note to it. Their conversation is archived in your Inbox.

### Working with a candidate

Click a candidate to open their profile over the pipeline. From there you can:

- Read their full background and relevant years of experience.
- See and edit their **Personalised intro** — the line Rose opens her outreach with — before it's sent.
- **Enrich email & phone** to find their contact details.
- Open their LinkedIn profile.

### Bulk actions

Select candidates to **Remove** them from the project or **Export** them to a spreadsheet. Exports are capped at a set number of candidates at a time; the app tells you if you go over.

## Adding more candidates

You can add candidates at any time — before launch or while the project is running — by asking Rose in the project's chat, pulling more from your [calibrated search](/finding-candidates/calibration), or using [Manual Filters](/finding-candidates/manual-filters). If a completed project gets new candidates, it resumes so they receive the sequence too.

## Launching

A draft project shows a **Launch project** button above the pipeline. You need at least one contactable candidate; Rose recommends ten or more. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching) for the full flow.
