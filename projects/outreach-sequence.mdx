title: The Outreach Sequence
description: Choose how outreach runs — email, LinkedIn, or both — then review every message Rose writes before anything sends.
---

The outreach sequence is the series of steps Rose sends each candidate. You decide its **shape** — which channels, in what order, with what gaps — and Rose writes the messages, personalised for every candidate.

## Step 1 — Shape the sequence

Before Rose writes a word, she asks **How should the outreach run?** with a small builder. It opens on the project's current sequence, so if you're happy with it you can confirm straight away.

### Start from a template

| Template | What it does |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Email only** | Three emails, spaced out. No LinkedIn. |
| **LinkedIn only** | An invite, a message once they accept, then an InMail. |
| **Email + LinkedIn** | An invite and a message on LinkedIn, then three emails. |

Each tab shows how many steps the template has. If you've run projects before, a **From a project** tab lets you reuse the shape of a sequence you've already used. It copies the steps and wait times — Rose still writes fresh messages for the new role.

### Adjust the steps

Drag blocks in from the tray (**Drag in, or click to add**), reorder them, remove them, and change the wait before each one.

| Block | What it sends |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Email** | An email from your connected mailbox |
| **LinkedIn invite** | A connection request, with no note |
| **LinkedIn message** | A direct message, sent once you're connected |
| **InMail** | Reaches anyone, uses your InMail credits |

Rose suggests what to add next — for example, a follow-up email for people who don't accept your invite. A few rules keep sequences sensible:

- Only **one LinkedIn invite** can go out per person.
- A LinkedIn message needs an invite before it. Anyone you aren't connected with gets an InMail instead.
- A sequence has a minimum and a maximum number of steps; the builder tells you if you're outside them.

When it looks right, click **Use this sequence**. The card shows **Sequence set**.

<Callout kind="info">
  LinkedIn steps send from **your own** LinkedIn account. Connect it on the [Integrations](/integrations/linkedin) page first — Rose will prompt you if you haven't.
</Callout>

## Step 2 — Review the messages

Rose then writes every step and shows you the sequence one tab per step. Open each step before approving — you can edit any subject or body inline, or click **Ask Rose to edit** and tell her what to change.

- **Tokens** such as the candidate's first name are personalised by Rose for every recipient.
- **Highlighted phrases** may hurt deliverability. Remove them yourself, or use **Fix all with Rose**. When you're clear you'll see *Spam check · you're in the clear.*

When every step looks right, click **Approve**.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Avoid hype (“revolutionary”, “once-in-a-lifetime”) and pressure (“last chance”, “urgent”). They land in spam and depress reply rates.
</Callout>

## The Outreach tab

Everything you approved lives on the project's **Outreach** tab, where you can review and edit the steps before launch.

The **Sending schedule** sets the days, hours and timezone emails can go out. Rose paces sends gradually and pauses automatically if emails start bouncing. Changes to the schedule apply to a live project immediately.

Rose stops a candidate's sequence as soon as they reply.
