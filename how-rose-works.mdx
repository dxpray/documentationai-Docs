Rose is an AI recruiter you work with in chat. You tell her who you're hiring; she plans the work with you, finds real candidates, writes and sends the outreach, and handles replies until someone is ready to book a call.

## Rose as a sourcing agent

You describe who you're looking for in plain language. Rose either **calibrates** a search with you — three candidates at a time, tuned by your verdicts — or, when you already know exactly who you want, runs a **real-time search** and groups the results into named clusters. Nobody is added to a project until you say so. See [Calibrating a Search](/finding-candidates/calibration).

## Rose as an outreach agent

Once you've shaped the outreach sequence and launched the project, Rose sends each step — emails from your own mailbox, LinkedIn invites and messages from your own LinkedIn — and responds to candidate replies: fielding questions, collecting availability, and flagging when someone is ready to be booked.

You choose how much of each conversation she runs:

- **Rose replies automatically** — end-to-end handling; Rose escalates only the tricky ones
- **Rose drafts, you approve** — Rose drafts every reply, but it goes out only after you approve it
- **You reply yourself** — Rose stays out of the thread

See [Your Level of Control](/how-rose-works/levels-of-control).

## The end-to-end workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Rose who you're hiring" icon="message-square" title-type="p">
    Describe the role or upload a job description. In **Planning** mode Rose turns it into a plan you approve first. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find and choose candidates" icon="users" title-type="p">
    Calibrate a search with Rose, or use [Manual Filters](/finding-candidates/manual-filters), and add the people you want to the project.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shape the outreach" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Choose the steps — email, LinkedIn, or both — and review every message. See [The Outreach Sequence](/projects/outreach-sequence).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch" icon="send" title-type="p">
    Rose reaches out and manages the conversation from there. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book interviews" icon="calendar" title-type="p">
    When a candidate is interested, Rose books the call or hands it to you, depending on your settings. See [Booking Interviews](/booking-interviews).
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full list of what Rose can do, see [What Rose Can Do](/how-rose-works/skills).
