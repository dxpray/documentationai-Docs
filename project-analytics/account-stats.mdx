---
title: Account-Level Stats
description: See your hiring performance rolled up across every project.
---

The top of the **Projects** page shows headline stats across your projects, so you can gauge overall performance at a glance.

| Stat | What it shows |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Active** | How many projects are currently in progress |
| **Reply rate** | The share of contacted candidates who have replied |
| **Interviews booked** | Total candidates with a booked interview |

These stats cover the projects you can see — the same ones in your Projects list — and leave out cancelled projects.

For a view centred on your calendar instead, the [Bookings page](/booking-interviews/the-bookings-page) breaks down interviews by Today, Tomorrow, This week, Confirmed, and Past.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Reply rate is the fastest signal of whether your targeting and messaging are landing. If it's low, revisit your [outreach sequence](/projects/outreach-sequence) or tighten who you're sourcing.
</Callout>
