---
title: The Project Funnel
description: The live pipeline bar inside each project shows how candidates move from shortlisted to booked.
---

At the top of a project's **Leads (CRM)** tab, a pipeline bar shows where every candidate stands. Each stage gets a coloured segment sized to how many candidates are in it, with a count underneath.

## What each stage means

| Stage | What it counts |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Shortlisted** | Candidates added to the project who haven't been contacted yet |
| **In Outreach** | Candidates who are receiving your outreach sequence and haven't replied |
| **Replied** | Candidates who have replied |
| **Interested** | Candidates who expressed positive interest |
| **Ready to book** | Candidates ready for an interview to be scheduled |
| **Interviews booked** | Candidates with a booked interview |

Three more stages sit outside the bar and are hidden by default: **Email not found**, **Not interested**, and **Rejected**. Use **Show stages** above the pipeline to reveal them. See [Managing a Project](/projects/managing-a-project).

## On the Projects page

Each project in your **Projects** list has a smaller version of the same bar in its **Pipeline** column, with a line such as "3 booked of 40". The **Replies** column shows how many candidates have replied and your reply rate for that project.

<Callout kind="info">
  These numbers update live as Rose works, so the bar always reflects the current state of your pipeline.
</Callout>
