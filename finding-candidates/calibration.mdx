---
title: Calibrating a Search
description: Teach Rose what a good match looks like by judging a few profiles at a time — then pull as many more as you need from a search you trust.
---

Calibration is how Rose learns what *you* mean by a good candidate. Rather than returning hundreds of profiles and hoping, she shows you three at a time, learns from your verdicts, and tunes the next three — until a whole round lands. The result is a **calibrated search** you can return to whenever the project needs more people.

## How a round works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rose shows three profiles" icon="users">
    Each round opens with a short note on what Rose looked for, then three candidates with their current role, location and relevant years of experience. Click a name to open the full profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You judge each one" icon="thumbs-up">
    Mark each profile **Good match**, **Not a match**, or **Unsure**. Add a reason if you like — *“What works about them?”* or *“What's off?”*. Reasons are optional, but they're the most useful signal you can give.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rose tunes the next round" icon="refresh-cw">
    Submit, and Rose uses your verdicts to refine the search. If everything looks right she'll suggest **Review search**; if not, **Refine search** brings back a new three.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  **Say why.** “Too junior”, “wrong industry” or “exactly this background” teaches Rose far more than a thumbs up or down on its own.
</Callout>

### Adding the people you like

The **Add liked candidates to project** switch is on by default: everyone you mark a good match is added to the project when you submit. Turn it off if you only want to train the search. Each new candidate added to a project uses credits.

## Accepting the search

When a round lands, Rose asks you to **Review this search** — a summary of the evidence from the profiles you approved. Choose **Use this search** and the project is marked **Search accepted**.

From then on, the chip at the top of the chat shows the project's calibrated search and roughly how many profiles are in the pool. Open it to see **Your calibrated search**:

- **Looking for** — the requirements Rose locked in.
- **What you told me** — the things you said directly.
- **What I picked up** — preferences Rose inferred from your verdicts. These affect ranking but never exclude anyone.

Two actions sit underneath:

- **Pull more** — add the next batch of candidates from the calibrated pool.
- **Recalibrate** — run new rounds if the brief has changed.

<Callout kind="info">
  A project that shows **Not calibrated** still works — it just hasn't been through a round yet. Click **Start calibrating** and Rose will show you a first three.
</Callout>

## Excluding people you've already sourced

When you start a new chat, use the **Exclude** control in the message box (next to the project picker) to stop familiar faces coming back:

- **Anyone already in a project**
- **Anyone I've already seen**
- **Or pick projects** — exclude only the candidates in specific projects.
