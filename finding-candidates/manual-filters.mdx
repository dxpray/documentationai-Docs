---
title: Manual Filters
description: Build a precise candidate search by hand across role, skills, location, experience, company, and education.
---

If you prefer to build a search yourself, use **Manual Search**. Open it from the link under the message box on the Rose home screen. The **Filters** panel on the left holds every filter; your results appear on the right.

## Building a search

Open a group, type a value, and press **Enter** to add it as a chip — many fields suggest matches as you type. Text fields marked **Include** / **Exclude** let you rule people out as well as in. When you've made changes, click **Apply filters** to run the search, or **Revert** to undo them. **Clear all** starts over.

## The filter groups

**Role & title** — Current title, past title, seniority (C-Level, Senior, Manager, Mid-Level, Specialist, Intern, Other), and function. You can also limit results to decision makers, or search by first or last name.

**Skills & keywords** — Skills and keywords from candidates' profiles, industry, and profile language. Three switches help you find people who may be more open to a move: **Recently changed jobs**, **Posts on LinkedIn**, and **In the news**.

**Location** — The candidate's region, and optionally the company's headquarters (**Company HQ**).

**Experience** — Years of experience, years at current company, and years in current position.

**Company** — Current and past company by name, domain, or LinkedIn URL, plus headcount and company type. Targeting specific companies is one of the most reliable ways to find relevant experience — see [Manual Filter Tips](/tips-and-tricks/manual-filters).

**Education** — School or university.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Title, company, and keyword fields can combine their chips with **AND** or **OR**. See [Boolean Search](/tips-and-tricks/boolean-search).
</Callout>

## Reading your results

The heading shows how many candidates match. Each card shows the candidate's name, headline, current role and employer, location, and education. If a candidate is already in one of your projects, the card tells you which. Click a card to open the full profile.

Select the candidates you want, then click **Add to Project** and pick the project. Adding to a live project enrols them in its existing outreach.

## Optimising your search

Aim for the sweet spot — precise enough to be relevant, but not so narrow you run out of candidates. Start broad and add filters one at a time, watching the count change.

- A pool in the hundreds to low thousands is a good starting point.
- If your pool is very large (tens of thousands), add more specific filters.
- If your pool is very small, relax one or two criteria.
