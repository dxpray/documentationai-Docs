Open **More → Integrations** in the left sidebar to see **Your Integrations**. Everything you've connected is listed at the top under **Connected**, and the tools you can add are grouped into sections below.

## In this section

- **[Mailboxes](/integrations/mailboxes)** — Gmail, Outlook, or any IMAP inbox. Rose sends outreach from these, so replies come back to you.
- **[LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin)** — reach candidates with connection requests and messages when email isn't enough.
- **[Scheduling](/integrations/scheduling)** — Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and Zoom.
- **[Interview Preferences](/integrations/interview-preferences)** — the rules Rose follows when booking.
- **[Applicant Tracking (ATS)](/integrations/applicant-tracking)** — push candidates to your ATS.
- **[Notifications](/integrations/notifications)** — Slack alerts when something needs you.

## Your plan

Some sections show what your plan includes — for example, how many mailboxes each team member can connect. If something isn't on your plan, its card shows **Upgrade to unlock**. See [Plans](/billing-and-subscription/plans).

<Callout kind="info">
  To connect a tool, click **Connect** on its card and follow the prompts. Connected tools can be disconnected at any time.
</Callout>
