---
title: How do I control how Rose replies?
description: Quick answers about reply modes, objectives and notifications.
---

## Where do I change how Rose replies on a project?

Open the project and go to the **Reply handling** tab. **Reply automation** sets who replies, and **Objective** sets what Rose works toward. See [Your Level of Control](/how-rose-works/levels-of-control).

## Does changing the objective change my outreach?

No. Your outreach messages go out exactly as written. Conversations switch from Rose's next reply. See [Reply Objectives](/how-rose-works/reply-objectives).

## Can I set the same reply settings for every new project?

Yes. Set your defaults in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings). Existing projects keep their own settings.

## Why didn't Rose book the call?

Rose books only when she replies automatically and the objective is **Ask for availability**. In other setups the times are saved and you're told, so you can book it yourself. See [How Rose Books Interviews](/booking-interviews/how-rose-books).

## How do I get a Slack message when a goal is met?

Connect [Slack](/integrations/notifications), then turn on **Post in Slack** in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings).
