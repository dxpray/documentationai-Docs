Getting up and running takes just a few minutes.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" icon="user-plus">
    Go to [clients.gethiros.com](https://clients.gethiros.com), enter your work email, and type in the verification code sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organisation" icon="building">
    Name your organisation — your company's workspace for projects, candidates, and billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar" icon="calendar">
    Link Google Calendar or Outlook (and Zoom, if you use it) so Rose can book intro calls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Introduce your company to Rose" icon="book-open">
    Give Rose your company website so she can tailor every search and message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan" icon="credit-card">
    Pick a plan, or skip for now and choose one later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full walkthrough, see [Create an Account](/getting-started/create-account).

## Start your first project

Open **Rose** from the left sidebar and describe who you're hiring in plain language — for example, *“a senior backend engineer in Berlin with fintech experience.”* Rose proposes a plan, asks for anything she still needs, and calibrates a search with you three candidates at a time. See [Getting Started](/getting-started).
