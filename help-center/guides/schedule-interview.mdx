---
title: Scheduling an interview
description: How interviews get booked in Hiros — automatically by Rose, or manually by you.
---

When a candidate is interested and shares their availability, Rose can book the intro call for you — or hand it over ready for you to confirm. How much Rose does depends on the **Scheduling Mode** in your [Interview Preferences](/integrations/interview-preferences).

## Book a call yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation" icon="inbox">
    In the [Inbox](/inbox), select the candidate who's ready to meet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Book interview" icon="calendar-plus">
    The **Book interview** button appears in the conversation once the candidate has shared their availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date and time" icon="clock">
    Choose from slots pulled from your connected calendar. Use **Change duration** if the call needs to be longer or shorter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" icon="check-circle">
    Review the details, optionally add notes for the candidate, and click **Confirm Booking**. The invite is added to your calendar (with a video link if Zoom is connected) and the candidate gets a confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let Rose book for you

Set your Scheduling Mode to **Automatic** and Rose books interviews on the first available slot — no action needed. Prefer to stay in control? **Semi-automatic** has Rose suggest times for you to confirm or change.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Rose needs Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar connected to book interviews. Set this up on the [Integrations](/integrations/scheduling) page.
</Callout>

Learn more in [Booking Interviews](/booking-interviews).
