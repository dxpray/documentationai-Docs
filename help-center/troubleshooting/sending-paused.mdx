---
title: My project stopped sending
description: What to do when a mailbox or LinkedIn account disconnects, or a project pauses.
---

If outreach stops, the project shows why. Work through the case that matches.

## Sending is paused: the mailbox was disconnected

A password change or revoked access can disconnect a mailbox. Rose pauses every project sending from it, and the owner gets an email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reconnect" icon="plug">
    Click **Reconnect mailbox** on the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume" icon="play">
    Click **Resume** on the project to carry on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the project owner can reconnect, because a project sends from its owner's accounts. See [Mailboxes](/integrations/mailboxes).

## Only LinkedIn steps are going out

No inbox is connected for the email steps. They wait until the project owner connects one.

## LinkedIn steps aren't sending

LinkedIn sessions expire from time to time. Click **Reconnect LinkedIn** on the Integrations page or in the chat, and the steps carry on. See [LinkedIn](/integrations/linkedin).

## The project says Paused

Someone paused it, or Rose did. Click **Resume** on the project page. See [Project Statuses](/projects/project-statuses).
