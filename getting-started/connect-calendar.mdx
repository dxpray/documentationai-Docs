---
title: Connect Your Calendar
description: Link Google Calendar, Outlook, or Zoom so Rose can book intro calls directly into your schedule.
---

Rose books intro calls directly into your calendar. During setup, the **Connect your calendar and email** screen lets you choose the platform your company uses.

## Your options

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Rose will book intro calls directly in your Google Calendar.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Outlook Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Rose will schedule meetings and send invites through Outlook.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Zoom" icon="video">
    Rose will create Zoom meetings and attach links to your bookings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Connecting

Click **Connect** next to your chosen platform and follow the authorisation prompts. Once it's linked, the option shows a **Connected** badge. You can connect more than one — for example, Outlook for your calendar and Zoom for meeting links.

**Continue** unlocks once Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar is connected — Zoom on its own isn't enough, because Rose needs a calendar to add interviews to. In a hurry? Choose **Skip for now** and connect later.

<Callout kind="info">
  You can connect, change, or disconnect calendars any time from **Integrations** (under **More** in the sidebar). Rose needs a connected calendar to book interviews. See [Scheduling Integrations](/integrations/scheduling).
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Using a personal Outlook account (such as @outlook.com or @hotmail.com)? Teams links aren't always added to invites automatically — connecting Zoom as well ensures a call link is always attached.
</Callout>

Your mailbox — the inbox Rose sends outreach from — is connected separately, when you first need it. See [Mailboxes](/integrations/mailboxes).
