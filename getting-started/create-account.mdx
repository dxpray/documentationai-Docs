Creating your account takes a couple of minutes. Hiros walks you through a short setup, and you can skip any optional step and come back to it later.

## Sign up

Go to [clients.gethiros.com](https://clients.gethiros.com) and enter your work email. Hiros sends a verification code to that address — enter it on screen to confirm it's you.

If you already have an account, enter the same email to sign back in.

## The setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organisation" icon="building">
    Your **organisation** is your company's workspace in Hiros. It holds your projects, candidates, integrations, and billing, and it's what you invite teammates into. Give it a name to create it. If you've been invited to an existing organisation, you'll join that instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar and email" icon="calendar">
    Connect Google Calendar or Outlook Calendar (and Zoom, if you use it) so Rose can book interviews. You can choose **Skip for now** and do this later. See [Connect Your Calendar](/getting-started/connect-calendar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Introduce your company to Rose" icon="book-open">
    Choose **Yes, add context**, drop in your company website, and Rose fills in the rest for you to check. Or choose **Maybe later**. See [Set Up Your Knowledge Base](/getting-started/knowledge-base).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan" icon="credit-card">
    Pick the plan that fits your hiring, or choose **Skip for now** to go straight to Rose. See [Plans](/billing-and-subscription/plans).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Work with multiple clients as a recruiter or agency? You can switch between organisations any time using the organisation switcher at the bottom of the left sidebar. See [Multi-User Management](/multi-user-management).
</Callout>

When you're through, you land on the Rose home screen, ready for [your first project](/getting-started).
