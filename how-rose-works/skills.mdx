---
title: What Rose Can Do
description: The eight skills Rose works with, and when to reach for each one.
---

Rose has eight skills. You rarely need them all at once — most work moves through three stages, or you can hand Rose the whole hire and she [plans it with you](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).

To see them in the app, click **Browse skills** under the message box on the Rose home screen. Each card shows what the skill does and an example prompt you can use straight away.

## Plan with Rose

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan the hire, step by step** | Turns a role into a step-by-step plan and runs it with you — every step waits for your approval before it moves. | *“Plan the hire for a senior product designer in Lisbon”* |

## Find people

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Talent pool calibration** | Describe the person you're after and Rose brings back three at a time. Mark each a good match or not — and say why — and the next three come back tuned to that. See [Calibration](/finding-candidates/calibration). | *“Find me engineers who've actually built card-issuing infrastructure”* |
| **Real-time search** | When you already know exactly who you want — named companies, a specific title, a fixed list — Rose searches live and groups the results into named clusters. | *“Search Stripe, Adyen and Checkout.com for senior backend engineers”* |
| **Company search** | Describe the kind of company you want to hire out of and Rose finds them — competitors, adjacent markets — then sources from the ones you pick. | *“Find me companies like Ramp, then source product engineers from them”* |

## Explore

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Size up the talent market** | Sizes talent pools, maps the company landscape, and gives honest strategy advice before you commit to anything. | *“How deep is the ML-infra talent pool in Paris?”* |

## Reach out and hand off

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft the outreach emails** | Turns a role or job description into a draft outreach sequence — every field and email editable, with spam-word checks before anything sends. |
| **Launch and run the campaign** | Adds or trims candidates, sets the send schedule and reply handling, walks you through approval, and launches. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching). |
| **Export candidates to a spreadsheet** | Pulls a project's candidates into a spreadsheet — everyone, or just the ones you name. Rose tells you the cost and who's missing an email or phone before you commit. |
