---
title: What Rose Can Do
description: The seventeen skills and three ways of working Rose offers, and when to reach for each one.
---

Rose has seventeen skills and three ways of working. You don't need to learn them: just tell Rose what you need, or hand her the whole hire and she [plans it with you](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).

To see them in the app, click **Browse skills** under the message box on the Rose home screen, or type **/** in the message box to pick one.

## Find people

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Talent pool calibration** | Shows you three people at a time and learns what a good match looks like from your calls. See [Calibration](/finding-candidates/calibration). | *“Find me engineers who've actually built card-issuing infrastructure”* |
| **Real-time search** | Searches live data against exact criteria, for when you already know who you want. | *“Run a real-time search, exactly as written: senior backend engineers at Stripe, Adyen or Checkout.com”* |
| **Auto shortlist** | Checks people from a search against your must-haves in the background, and shortlists only those who pass. Rolling out. See [Auto Mode](/how-rose-works/auto-mode). | *“Shortlist 20 data engineers who have worked at a scale-up”* |
| **Refine a search** | Adjusts a search you've already run when the results aren't quite right. | *“More from early-stage scale-ups, fewer from big banks”* |

## Research

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Work from a job description** | Reads a job description and turns it into the brief Rose searches from. | *“Here's the job description — find me people for this role”* |
| **Size up the talent market** | Sizes the talent pool and maps the market before you commit to anything. | *“How deep is the ML-infra talent pool in Paris?”* |
| **Find companies to hire from** | Finds the kind of company you want to hire out of, then sources people from the ones you pick. | *“Find me companies like Ramp, then source product engineers from them”* |

## Projects

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Set up a project** | Creates a project to hold the people, the outreach and the settings for one role. | *“Set up a project for this role — I'll paste the job description”* |
| **Add people to a project** | Adds the people you pick to a project, even one that's already sending. | *“Add the people I marked as a good match to my project”* |
| **Clean up a shortlist** | Takes people off a project by a rule you give, such as too junior, too many from one company, or companies to leave out. | *“Remove everyone with under five years of experience”* |
| **Check on a project** | Tells you where a project stands and what still stands between it and launch. | *“How is my project doing — is it ready to launch?”* |
| **Import candidates from a CSV** | Adds candidates to a project from a CSV of LinkedIn profile URLs. See [Importing Candidates](/finding-candidates/importing-candidates). | *“Import candidates from a CSV into this project”* |
| **Export candidates to a spreadsheet** | Pulls a project's candidates into a spreadsheet — everyone, or just the ones you name. | *“Export everyone who replied in this project”* |

## Outreach

| Skill | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft the outreach** | Writes the outreach for a project — emails, and LinkedIn steps if you use them — with every word editable. | *“Draft the outreach: an email, a LinkedIn connection request, then a follow-up email”* |
| **Connect inbox and schedule** | Sets who the outreach comes from and when it goes out. | *“Send only on weekdays, 9 to 5 Paris time”* |
| **Handle replies** | Sets how replies are handled, and what Rose works toward once someone answers. See [Reply Objectives](/how-rose-works/reply-objectives). | *“Draft my replies and ask interested people for their availability”* |
| **Launch the campaign** | Starts sending once the people, the outreach and the settings are in place. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching). | *“Launch this project”* |

## Ways of working

| Mode | What it does | Try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Planning** | Rose works out the plan for a hire with you, and acts on none of it while you're in Planning. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode). | *“Build me a hiring plan for two backend engineers”* |
| **Normal** | Rose works with you step by step, and asks before every change. | *“Find me senior product designers in Berlin”* |
| **Auto** | Rose builds your shortlist in the background, checking each person against your must-haves. Rolling out. See [Auto Mode](/how-rose-works/auto-mode). | *“Find me 30 senior backend engineers in London”* |
