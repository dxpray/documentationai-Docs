---
title: Reply Objectives
description: Tell Rose what to work toward once a candidate replies — availability, a scheduling link, an application page, or a phone number.
---

An **objective** is what Rose steers a conversation toward after a candidate replies. Your opening message and follow-ups go out exactly as written, and follow-ups stop as soon as a candidate replies. From there, Rose works toward the objective. When it's met, she steps back and you pick it up.

## The four objectives

| Objective | What Rose does | What you need |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Ask for availability** | Asks for two or three times that suit them. | Nothing extra. This is the default. |
| **Send scheduling link** | Sends your link so they can book into your calendar. | The project owner's scheduling link, set in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings). |
| **Application page** | Sends them to apply formally. | An application page link starting with `https://`. |
| **Ask for phone number** | Moves the conversation to a call by asking for their number. | Nothing extra. |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Can't choose **Send scheduling link**? The project owner needs to add their link in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings) first.
</Callout>

## How objectives work with reply modes

| Reply mode | Objective |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Rose replies automatically** | Required |
| **Rose drafts, you approve** | Optional |
| **You reply yourself** | Off |

See [Your Level of Control](/how-rose-works/levels-of-control) for what each mode does.

## Where to set it

- **For new projects** — set your defaults in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings).
- **While setting up outreach** — Rose asks *What should Rose work toward after a reply?* in the chat.
- **On a running project** — open the project's **Reply handling** tab and click **Change objective**.

## Changing the objective on a live project

The **Change objective** window shows who the change reaches before you apply it:

- Candidates who haven't replied yet get the new objective when they reply.
- Ongoing conversations switch from Rose's next reply.
- Your outreach messages don't change.

Tick **Also ask candidates who already reached it** if you want Rose to work toward the new objective with them too. Leave it unticked to use it for everyone else only.

## When the objective is met

Rose steps back and you can pick the conversation up. The project owner is told once per candidate, by email and in Slack according to their [notification settings](/how-rose-works/rose-settings), with what was shared: the times the candidate gave, the link Rose sent, or their phone number.

<Callout kind="info">
  With **Ask for availability** on a project where Rose replies automatically, a met objective can also lead to a booking. See [How Rose Books Interviews](/booking-interviews/how-rose-books).
</Callout>

## Seeing the objective

The objective appears as a **Goal** chip in the message box of a project's chat and in the Inbox thread header. The Inbox chip is hidden when you reply yourself.
