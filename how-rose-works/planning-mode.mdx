---
title: Planning Mode
description: Hand Rose the whole hire. She writes a step-by-step plan, you approve it, and she runs it with you one step at a time.
---

Planning mode is the easiest way to run a whole hire with Rose. Instead of asking for one thing at a time, you describe the role and Rose turns it into a plan — from understanding the role through to reaching out — that you read, adjust, and approve before anything happens.

## Normal and Planning

The composer has a **Mode** switch with two settings:

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Planning** | *Write a plan — Rose won't act.* Rose thinks the hire through with you and proposes a plan, but takes no actions while you're in this mode. |
| **Normal** | *Rose works with you, step by step.* Rose runs searches, adds candidates, and sets up outreach as you ask. |

New chats start in **Planning**. A chat that already has an approved plan reopens in **Normal**, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Reading the plan

When you brief Rose in Planning mode, she replies with a plan card. Steps are grouped into the stages of a hire:

- **Understand the role**
- **Explore the market**
- **Find candidates**
- **Build the shortlist**
- **Reach out**

Each group shows how many steps it holds. Open a group to see its steps, or use **Show all steps** to read the whole plan. Where a step involves a search, Rose adds a one-line note on the companies she plans to look at, so you can check her assumptions before she starts.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Want something different? Edit a step's wording with the pencil icon, or just tell Rose in chat — she'll propose a revised plan.
</Callout>

## Approving the plan

When the plan looks right, click **Run with me**. Rose switches the chat to **Normal** so she can act, and starts on the first step.

As she works, the plan becomes a live checklist:

- a **green check** marks a finished step,
- a **pulsing copper dot** marks the step she's working on,
- a quiet circle marks what's still to come.

A **plan panel** keeps your progress in view — *Step 2 of 5 · 40% complete* — and lets you jump to any stage in the chat.

## You approve each step

A plan is not a blank cheque. Rose still checks in with you at every decision that matters: she shows you profiles before adding anyone to a project, asks you to confirm the outreach sequence, and waits for your approval before launching. Approving the plan never authorises her to add candidates you haven't asked for.

<Callout kind="info">
  Prefer to drive yourself? Switch to **Normal** at any time and give Rose one instruction at a time. Planning mode is a shortcut, not a requirement.
</Callout>
