Rose is built to be trusted, but trust is earned on your terms. There are two dials: how Rose handles **candidate replies** on each project, and whether she **plans first or acts** in chat.

## Candidate replies

For every project you choose how much of each conversation Rose runs on her own. You can change it any time — even after the project has launched.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Rose replies automatically" icon="bot">
    End-to-end handling. Rose manages the full conversation, sending replies herself and escalating only the tricky ones. The fastest, most hands-off option.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rose drafts, you approve" icon="pen-line">
    Rose writes every reply, but nothing is sent until you approve it in the Inbox. Drafts show **Draft ready**, with **Approve & Send**, **Edit**, and **Reject**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="You reply yourself" icon="user">
    Rose stays out of the thread — every reply comes from you. Rose still sends the outreach sequence and tracks the pipeline.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Where to set it

- **For every new project** — set your defaults in [Settings › Rose](/how-rose-works/rose-settings). Without them, new projects start with **Rose drafts, you approve**.
- **When you launch a project** — Rose asks how **Candidate replies** should be handled before you go live. See [Launching a Project](/projects/launching).
- **Any time afterwards** — open the project and find **Reply automation** on the **Reply handling** tab.

Alongside the mode, you choose what Rose works toward after a reply. See [Reply Objectives](/how-rose-works/reply-objectives).

In the [Inbox](/inbox), each conversation shows a small badge — **Auto**, **Semi-auto**, or **Manual** — so you always know who's driving.

## Planning, Normal and Auto

The second dial sits in the message box on the Rose home screen. The **Mode** switch decides how much Rose does before checking in:

- **Planning** — Rose thinks the hire through with you and proposes a plan, but takes no actions. New chats start here.
- **Normal** — Rose works with you step by step, and asks before every change.
- **Auto** — Rose plans it with you, then works through it, checking in only where you've asked. Rolling out. See [Auto Mode](/how-rose-works/auto-mode).

In Normal mode, Rose shows you profiles before adding anyone, asks you to confirm the outreach sequence, and waits for your go-ahead before launching. See [Planning Mode](/how-rose-works/planning-mode).
