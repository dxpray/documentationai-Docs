In **Auto**, Rose plans the hire with you, then works through it. You decide where she stops to ask.

<Callout kind="info">
  Auto is rolling out to workspaces gradually. If you don't see it in the **Mode** switch yet, nothing is missing.
</Callout>

## Starting a run

Choose **Auto** in the **Mode** switch in the message box, then describe who you want. When you send your first message, Rose asks **Start with this setup?** so you can check the mode, the project and who's excluded.

Rose then proposes a plan. Each step shows **Go ahead** or **Ask me first**, and the plan says where she'll stop. She only does the steps in the plan you approve. Anything left out, such as the launch, stays undone until you ask.

## Where Rose checks with you

| Check-in | When it's on | When it's off |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Candidate checks** | You press **Find** | The search starts on its own |
| **Shortlist** | You say when to add | Rose adds them on her own |
| **Project** | You confirm the summary | Rose creates it on her own |
| **Emails** | You approve the sequence | Rose writes them on her own |
| **Launch** | You say when to launch | Rose launches on her own |

Every check-in starts on. To change your defaults for new Auto chats, open your profile menu and choose **Rose**. Each chat's plan can still change them for that chat.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Launch emails real people. Credits, connecting a mailbox or LinkedIn, and deleting anything always need you.
</Callout>

When Rose goes ahead without asking, the run's timeline says so, so you can see what she did.

## Building the shortlist

Rose shows the **Checks** she'll use: what she'll search for, and what every person must show.

- **Must be a clear yes** — people need to pass this.
- **Rules someone out** — people who show this are left out.
- **Nice to have** — ranks people higher, and never rules them out.

Rose reads titles, employers, dates and education on each profile. If she can't check something, she says so and offers an alternative. Choose how many people you want, from 1 to 100, and press **Find**.

The run works in the background, and its progress shows in the sidebar too. You can **Stop** at any time.

## When the run finishes

You get the shortlist, why Rose picked each person, and anyone who came close. From there you can **Find 10 more**, **Keep going**, **Widen the search**, or add them to a project. Adding people to a project uses credits as usual.

A run can also finish early, because the search ran out of people, or it reached the profile limit or time limit. Larger plans check more profiles per run.

## Auto and your other modes

- **Planning** — write a plan together, and Rose acts on none of it.
- **Normal** — Rose works step by step and asks before every change.
- **Auto** — Rose plans it with you, then works through it.
