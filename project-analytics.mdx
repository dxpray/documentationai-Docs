---
title: Project Analytics
description: Track performance across your pipeline — from first contact to interview booked — for each project and across all of them.
---

Hiros gives you two levels of analytics: a live pipeline bar inside each project, and rolled-up stats across all of your projects.

- **[The Project Funnel](/project-analytics/the-funnel)** — the pipeline bar on a project's **Leads (CRM)** tab.
- **[Account-Level Stats](/project-analytics/account-stats)** — the totals across every project you can see, on the **Projects** page.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Treat your **Not interested** responses as market intelligence — they often reveal how the market sees the role, the compensation, or the company.
</Callout>
